The night at the old harbor was wrapped in a thick, chilly sea breeze.

Lanterns swayed gently along the wooden pier, casting dim golden reflections upon the water.

Rian stood near the edge of the dock, watching the weathered cargo vessel prepare for departure.

That ship had been his home and workplace for the past five consecutive years.

Tonight marked his very last evening in the city before setting sail toward the far east.

Crew members were still bustling across the deck, securing heavy ropes and inspecting final cargo crates.

The deep hum of the engine echoed through the air, vibrating through the timber beneath his feet.

Farther out, soft waves crashed against the wooden pillars in a slow, rhythmic cadence.

He pulled his heavy jacket tighter, trying to shield himself from the biting midnight cold.

Taking a deep breath, he recalled every memory and farewell that had ever taken place here.

No final words were spoken as the massive anchor was slowly lifted from the harbor floor.

The stillness of the night felt heavy, as if the entire world were holding its breath.

An endless ocean stretched ahead, harboring secrets and brand new journeys yet to be discovered.

High above the dark horizon, the nocturnal sky watched the quiet farewell unfold without a word.

the moon was silent, while the ship slowly disappeared.

Rian remained standing there until the silhouette of the vessel completely merged into the black darkness.

Only the sound of the ocean wind and faint white foam were left behind on the surface.
